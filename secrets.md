### **Recipes and variations**

#### *Muesli (Almond + Cranberry)*

 **Base Recipe (Dry):**
- Pour 1 cup muesli into a bowl
- Eat as-is or with a spoon of soaked nuts on the side

 **Variations:**
- **Cold Coffee Muesli:** Pour your cold coffee over muesli. Let sit 5 mins. Crunchy + caffeinated.
- **Muesli Yogurt Parfait:** Layer muesli → thick curd → banana slices → honey. Repeat. Eat like a dessert.
- **Warm Milk Muesli:** Heat 1 cup milk, pour over muesli. Let sit 3 mins. Comforting for winters/monsoons.
- **Muesli Topping:** Sprinkle on peanut butter toast or smoothie bowl for extra crunch.

---
#### *Butter / Peanut Butter Toast*

**Base Recipe:**
- Toast 2 bread slices until golden brown
- Spread butter or peanut butter generously
- Optional: Sprinkle a pinch of salt (for PB) or sugar (for butter)

**Variations:**
- **Dark Chocolate PB Toast:** Spread peanut butter, then grate dark chocolate on top. The heat melts the chocolate slightly.
- **PB + Banana Toast:** Slice banana on top of peanut butter. Drizzle honey if you want extra sweetness.
- **Butter + Sugar Toast:** Old-school classic. Butter + sprinkle of sugar while bread is hot.
- **PB + Chia Sprinkle:** Spread PB, sprinkle soaked chia seeds + halim on top for omega-3 boost.
- **Cheese Toast:** Butter on outer side, cheese slice inside, grill on pan until cheese melts.

#### *Overnight Mocha Oats*

**Base Recipe:**
- 1/2 cup rolled oats
- 1/2 cup milk
- 3-4 teaspoons of warm water + coffee mixture
- 2 tbsp of dark chocolate peanut butter
- Pinch of salt
- 1 tsp sugar

**Method:**
1. Mix all ingredients in a jar.
2. Stir well. Seal and refrigerate overnight (or at least 4 hours).
3. In the morning, stir again. Eat cold or warm slightly in microwave.

**Variations:**
- **Add chia seeds:** 1 tsp chia seeds mixed in for extra thickness and omega-3
- **Top with banana:** Slice banana on top before eating for natural sweetness
- **Sprinkle muesli:** Add crunch on top just before eating

---
#### *Peanut Butter Banana Smoothie Bowl*

**Base Recipe:**
- 1 frozen banana (peel and freeze overnight)
- 1 tbsp peanut butter
- 1/4 cup milk
- 1 tsp cocoa powder (optional)

**Method:**
1. Blend all ingredients until thick and creamy.
2. Pour into a bowl.
3. Top with muesli, soaked nuts, chia seeds, or diced fruit.

**Variations:**
- **Chocolate Smoothie Bowl:** Add extra cocoa powder + a few dark chocolate chips on top
- **Coffee Smoothie Bowl:** Add 2 tbsp cold coffee instead of milk for a mocha kick

#### Eggs (Omelette / Fried / Bhurji)

**Base Recipe (Omelette):**
- 2 eggs
- Pinch of salt + pepper
- 1 tbsp oil/butter for pan
**Method:**
1. Crack eggs into a bowl. Beat well with fork until frothy.
2. Heat pan on medium. Add oil/butter.
3. Pour eggs. Let set for 1 min.
4. Fold or flip. Cook another 30 seconds. Serve.

**Base Recipe (Sunny Side Up):**
1. Heat pan. Add oil/butter.
2. Crack egg directly into pan.
3. Cook on low heat until whites set but yolk is runny (3-4 mins).
4. Season with salt + pepper. Serve on toast.

**Base Recipe (Egg Bhurji):**
1. Heat oil in pan. Add jeera, chopped onion, green chili. Sauté 2 mins.
2. Add chopped tomato, turmeric, salt. Cook 1 min.
3. Crack 2 eggs directly into pan. Stir vigorously until scrambled and cooked.
4. Pile onto buttered toast. Top with coriander.

**Variations:**
- **Cheese Omelette:** Add grated cheese before folding
- **Veggie Omelette:** Add chopped onions, capsicum, tomato to beaten eggs
- **Masala Omelette:** Add chili powder, turmeric, coriander to beaten eggs

**Cracking Tip:** Tap egg on flat counter (not pan edge). Pull apart gently with thumbs. Crack into a small bowl first, then slide into pan to avoid shell fragments.

#### *Masala Oats*

**Base Recipe:**
- 1/2 cup oats
- 1 cup water
- 1/4 cup chopped veggies (onion, tomato, peas, carrot)
- 1 tsp oil/ghee
- 1/4 tsp mustard seeds, 1/4 tsp jeera, pinch of hing
- 1/4 tsp turmeric, 1/2 tsp chili powder, salt to taste
- Curry leaves (optional)

**Method:**
1. Heat oil/ghee in pan. Add mustard seeds, jeera, hing, curry leaves. Let splutter.
2. Add chopped onion, veggies. Sauté 2-3 mins.
3. Add turmeric, chili powder, salt. Mix.
4. Add oats + water. Stir continuously on medium heat.
5. Cook 3-4 mins until oats absorb water and become creamy.
6. Top with coriander. Serve hot.

**Variations:**
- **Curd Masala Oats:** Replace half the water with buttermilk/chaas for a tangy, risotto-like texture
- **Tomato Masala Oats:** Replace water with tomato puree for a rich, tangy version
- **Lemon Coriander Oats:** Squeeze fresh lemon + lots of coriander before serving
- **Egg Masala Oats:** Crack an egg into the oats while cooking and stir. Extra protein, no extra pan.

#### *Upma*

**Base Recipe:**
- 1/2 cup rava/suji
- 1.5 cups water
- 1 tbsp ghee/oil
- 1/4 tsp mustard seeds, 1/4 tsp urad dal, 1/4 tsp chana dal, pinch of hing
- 1 onion chopped, 1 green chili, few curry leaves
- 1/4 tsp turmeric, salt to taste
- Optional: 1/4 cup peas, 1 small carrot chopped

**Method:**
1. **Dry roast rava** in a pan on low flame for 5-7 mins until it smells nutty and changes color slightly. Set aside.
2. Heat ghee/oil in same pan. Add mustard seeds, urad dal, chana dal, hing. Let splutter.
3. Add curry leaves, green chili, onion, veggies. Sauté 3-4 mins.
4. Add water, salt, turmeric. Bring to a boil.
5. **Lower flame.** Slowly add roasted rava while stirring continuously to avoid lumps.
6. Cover and cook on low for 2-3 mins. Fluff with fork. Serve hot with chutney or pickle.

#### *Paneer Bhurji Toast*

**Base Recipe:**
- 200g paneer, crumbled
- 2 onion finely chopped
- 2 tomato finely chopped
- 3 green chili chopped
- 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp chili powder, salt, pepper
- 1 tbsp oil/ghee
- bread slices, buttered and toasted

**Method:**
1. Heat oil in pan. Add onion + green chili. Sauté until soft (3 mins).
2. Add tomato, turmeric, chili powder, salt and pepper. Cook until tomato softens (2 mins).
3. Add crumbled paneer and mix well. Cook 2 mins.
4. Pile mixture onto buttered toast. Top with coriander. Serve hot.

**Variations:**
- **Cheesy Paneer Bhurji:** Add grated cheese on top of paneer while it's still hot
- **Paneer Bhurji Roll:** Stuff inside a roti/chapati instead of toast for a wrap
- **Spicy Paneer Bhurji:** Add extra green chilies + chaat masala before serving
- **Bell Pepper Paneer Bhurji:** Add chopped capsicum with onions for crunch

#### *Wheat Flour Dosa + Peanut Chutney*

**Base Recipe (Dosa):**
- 1 cup wheat flour (atta)
- 1 cup water (or as needed)
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/4 tsp jeera (optional)
- 1 tbsp finely chopped onion + coriander (optional)
- Oil/ghee for cooking

**Method:**
1. Mix wheat flour, salt, jeera, onion, coriander with water. Whisk until smooth batter (pouring consistency like buttermilk).
2. Let rest 10 mins.
3. Heat non-stick tawa/pan on medium-high. Sprinkle a few drops of water. Wipe dry.
4. Pour a ladle of batter. Spread in circular motion to make a thin crepe.
5. Drizzle oil/ghee on edges. Cook 1-2 mins until crispy and golden.
6. Flip if needed. Cook 30 seconds. Fold and serve.

**Base Recipe (Peanut Chutney):**
- 1/2 cup roasted peanuts
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1 dried red chili (or 1/2 tsp chili powder)
- Salt to taste
- Water as needed
- Optional Tadka: 1 tsp oil, 1/4 tsp mustard seeds, few curry leaves

**Method:**
1. Blend peanuts + garlic + chili + salt + water until smooth paste.
2. Heat oil. Add mustard seeds + curry leaves. Let splutter.
3. Pour tadka over chutney. Mix. Serve.

**Variations:**
- **Peanut + Curd Chutney:** Mix peanut chutney with fresh curd for a creamy dip

**Prep Tip:** Make batter the night before. It slightly ferments and gives a better texture. Or make fresh — only takes 10 mins.

#### Besan Chilla + Green Chutney

**Base Recipe (Chilla):**
- 1 cup besan (gram flour)
- 1/2 cup water (or as needed)
- 1/4 tsp turmeric, 1/2 tsp chili powder, 1/4 tsp ajwain (carom seeds), salt
- 1 onion finely chopped, 1 tomato finely chopped, 2 tbsp coriander

**Method:**
1. Mix besan + water + spices + salt. Whisk until smooth batter (slightly thicker than dosa batter).
2. Add chopped onion, tomato, coriander. Mix.
3. Heat non-stick pan. Grease lightly.
4. Pour ladle of batter. Spread gently into a circle (slightly thick).
5. Drizzle oil on edges. Cook 2-3 mins on medium until golden.
6. Flip carefully. Cook another 1-2 mins. Serve with chutney.

**Base Recipe (Green Chutney):**
- 1 cup coriander leaves
- 1/4 cup mint leaves (optional)
- 2 green chillies
- 1/2 inch ginger
- 2 garlic pods
- 1 tsp lemon juice
- Oil
- 1/4 tsp sugar
- Salt to taste
- Water as needed

**Method:**
1. Blend all ingredients with minimal water until smooth paste.
2. Adjust salt and lemon. Serve fresh.

**Variations:**
- **Paneer Stuffed Chilla:** Sprinkle crumbled paneer on chilla while cooking. Fold and serve.
- **Cheese Chilla:** Grate cheese on top while chilla is hot
- **Onion-Mirchi Chilla:** Extra onions + green chilies for spicy version
- **Chilla Roll:** Spread green chutney on cooked chilla. Roll it up. Eat like a wrap.
- **Vegetable Chilla:** Add grated carrot, capsicum, spinach to batter

**Prep Tip:** Make green chutney on Sunday night. Store in airtight container in fridge. Good for 3-4 days.
